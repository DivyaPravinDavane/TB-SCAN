WHO & NTEP Chest Radiography TB Triage Protocol

Guidelines for Computer-Aided Detection (CAD) in Active TB Case Finding

1

Digital CXR Screening & CAD Score

Adults and adolescents presenting with cough > 2 weeks or epidemiological risk factors undergo digital chest radiography with AI CAD scoring.

2

Risk Stratification Triage

Patients with AI CAD Score ≥ 60% (High Risk) are immediately prioritized and isolated for rapid molecular sputum diagnostic testing.

3

Molecular Microbiologic Confirmation (GeneXpert MTB/RIF)

Perform GeneXpert or Ultra on high-risk individuals to confirm Mycobacterium tuberculosis complex DNA and detect Rifampicin resistance.

4

Physician Clinical Sign-Off & ATT Initiation

Document radiologist verification, correlate with clinical signs, and initiate appropriate First-Line or Drug-Resistant Anti-TB Regimen.
